# SOURCE: GenTac 2026

## Paper
**GenTac: Generative Modeling and Forecasting of Soccer Tactics**

Correct identifier: arXiv:2604.11786.

Authors verified in the primary source: Jiayuan Rao, Tianlin Gui, Haoning Wu, Yanfeng Wang, Weidi Xie.

## Relevance
1. Forecasts multi-player trajectories from structured tracking data.
2. Grounds future behavior in a 15-class tactical event space.
3. Does not use football video as the visual modality for BAA.
4. Does not establish the same temporally localized SoccerNet-style ball-action anticipation setup.
5. It strongly weakens generic tactical-forecasting novelty.
