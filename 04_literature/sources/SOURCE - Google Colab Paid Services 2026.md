# Source: Google Colab Paid Services Pricing

## Source class
Official Google Colab pricing page.

## Verified at

## Claims supported
1. Colab offers free access plus paid options.
2. Colab Pro is described as including 100 compute units per month at this verification checkpoint.
3. Compute units expire after 90 days.
4. GPU types and resource availability can vary, and usage limits still apply.

## Use in this project
