Source: SoccerRAG, 2024

Primary paper: SoccerRAG, Multimodal Soccer Information Retrieval via
Natural Queries.

Verified claims

1.  Proposes natural-language retrieval over an augmented
    SoccerNet-based multimodal dataset.
2.  The described information includes video or image frames, audio,
    transcribed captions, game events, and player information.
3.  The framework combines database-backed information with
    natural-language querying.
4.  The paper reports limitations around complex queries and large data
    volumes.
5.  Future work includes retrieving specific clips and adding deeper
    video understanding tasks.

Project implication

A generic “RAG for football video” thesis is not sufficiently
distinctive. A retrieval project must target a more specific unresolved
problem such as tactical, spatial, temporal, player-aware, or game-state
retrieval.
