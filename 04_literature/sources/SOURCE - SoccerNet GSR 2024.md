Source: SoccerNet Game State Reconstruction, 2024

Primary paper: Vladimir Somers et al., CVPR Workshops 2024.

Verified claims

1.  Formalizes Game State Reconstruction for football video.
2.  SoccerNet-GSR contains 200 video sequences of 30 seconds.
3.  The dataset includes pitch and camera-calibration annotations plus
    athlete pitch positions with role, team, and jersey number.
4.  The paper introduces the GS-HOTA evaluation metric and releases an
    end-to-end baseline.
5.  The paper explicitly connects reconstructed player identity and
    location to downstream insights including team tactics.
