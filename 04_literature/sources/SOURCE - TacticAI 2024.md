# SOURCE: TacticAI 2024

## Paper
**TacticAI: an AI assistant for football tactics**

Nature Communications 2024. DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-45965-x.

## Relevance
1. Uses explicit player positions, velocities, team information, and graph-based representations.
2. Restricted to corner-kick tactical situations.
3. Predicts outcomes such as likely receivers and shot-related outcomes.
4. Does not use video as the main visual input.
5. Does not solve open-play temporally localized BAA.

## Novelty consequence
Do not claim that player geometry or GNN-based football interaction modeling is new.
