Verified relevant tasks

The 2026 challenge program includes Ball Action Anticipation, Visual
Question Answering, Player-Centric Ball Action Spotting, and other
football AI tasks.

The Ball Action Anticipation challenge describes predicting timing and
type of ball-related actions within the next five seconds using
preceding gameplay video.

Project implication

Football video understanding remains an active benchmark area, so
specialization alone does not establish novelty.
