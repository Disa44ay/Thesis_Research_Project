# SOURCE: TacticGen 2026

## Paper
**TacticGen: Grounding Adaptable and Scalable Generation of Football Tactics**

Identifier: arXiv:2604.18210.

## Relevance
1. Uses very large professional tracking and event data for tactical trajectory generation.
2. Demonstrates that generic future tactical generation is already a heavily resourced research direction.
3. It is not the same as public-data multimodal BAA.

## Decision effect
Generic tactical generation or broad trajectory forecasting is not a preferred undergraduate novelty target.
