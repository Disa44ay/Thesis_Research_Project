# SOURCE - Beyond Pixels 2025

**Title:** Beyond Pixels: Leveraging the Language of Soccer to Improve Spatio-Temporal Action Detection in Broadcast Videos  
**Authors:** Jeremie Ochin, Raphael Chekroun, Bogdan Stanciulescu, Sotiris Manitsaris  
**arXiv:** 2505.09455

## Verified relevance
The paper processes noisy player-centric action detections together with clean game-state information using a Transformer-based sequence transduction model. It reasons over extended temporal context and team-level dynamics to improve observed-action detection/denoising.

## Threat to novelty
It further weakens any claim that long-range game-state reasoning, team-level dynamics, or structured context for football action understanding is itself novel.

## What remains open for this thesis
The target is still observed-action detection/denoising rather than class-and-time prediction for actions occurring only in an unobserved future interval.
