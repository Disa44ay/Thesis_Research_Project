Dataset: SoccerNet Ball Action Anticipation

Introduced with the 2025 FAANTRA work and based on SoccerNet Ball Action
Spotting.

Verified task framing

Predict future ball-related actions in unobserved future frames using
five-second or ten-second anticipation windows in the 2025 paper.

Open checks

1.  exact current download procedure,
2.  storage and preprocessing cost,
3.  exact inputs released with the development kit,
4.  compatibility with external or reconstructed game-state features,
5.  licensing and redistribution restrictions relevant to deployment
    demonstrations.
