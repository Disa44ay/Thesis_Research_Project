# Dataset: SoccerNet Ball Action Anticipation

Introduced with the 2025 FAANTRA work and based on SoccerNet Ball Action Spotting.

## Verified task framing
Predict future ball-related actions in unobserved future frames using five-second or ten-second anticipation windows in the 2025 paper.
