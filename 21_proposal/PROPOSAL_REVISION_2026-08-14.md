Proposal Revision

What changed

The first detailed proposal was technically comprehensive but judged too
long, messy, and disoriented for an instructor-facing research proposal.

It was not deleted. The long PDF, LaTeX source, bibliography, and
vault-native [[PROPOSAL_DRAFT]] remain preserved as historical material.

A shorter proposal was then created with the argument organized around:

1.  problem,
2.  prior work and gap,
3.  research questions,
4.  dataset and benchmark,
5.  method/system architecture,
6.  validation and feasibility,
7.  contributions, schedule, and references.

The revised proposal was separately audited against primary papers and
official project/platform documentation. The resulting evidence record
is [[CITATION_AND_SOURCE_AUDIT]].
