# Rejection: Exact 30 Second Future Gameplay Prediction

## Rejected framing
Predict the exact next thirty seconds of football gameplay.

## Reason
The target is too stochastic and too difficult to evaluate cleanly for the current thesis constraints. Existing football action anticipation work already treats five-second and ten-second future windows as a challenging research task.

## What survived
Short-horizon prediction of actions, coarse tactical states, possession outcomes, or zone transitions remains in scope when objectively measurable.
