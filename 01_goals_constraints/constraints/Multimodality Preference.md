Constraint: Multimodality Preference

Multimodality is a strong preference, not a hard requirement.

The motivation is to pursue contemporary, publication-relevant work
rather than simple incremental research. However, the project must not
add a modality only to claim that it is multimodal.

A unimodal topic may outrank a multimodal topic when its gap is more
defensible, specific, and feasible.
