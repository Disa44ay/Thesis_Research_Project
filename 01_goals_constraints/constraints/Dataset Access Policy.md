# Constraint: Dataset Access Policy

Preferred order:
1. free and directly downloadable public dataset,
2. free academic dataset requiring registration or request, only when access delay is unlikely to block the thesis and a fallback exists,
3. reject paid, private, uncertain-license, or partnership-dependent data.

The project cannot depend on an approval process with uncertain timing when the thesis schedule is short.
