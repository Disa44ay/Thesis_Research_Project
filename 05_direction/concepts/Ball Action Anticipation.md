# Concept: Ball Action Anticipation

Predict future ball-related football actions before they occur.

The 2025 FAANTRA formulation uses five-second and ten-second anticipation windows. The 2026 SoccerNet challenge uses a five-second future window after preceding gameplay video.

This makes short-horizon action anticipation a recognized research task and provides a more defensible target than attempting exact prediction of thirty seconds of stochastic gameplay.
