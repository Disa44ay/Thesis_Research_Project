Concept: Tactical Retrieval

Search football video or reconstructed game states using tactical,
spatial, temporal, player-aware, or compositional queries.

The research opportunity is not generic natural-language RAG over
football metadata. Existing SoccerRAG work already demonstrates
multimodal soccer information retrieval with natural queries.

The candidate research question is whether explicit spatiotemporal
game-state representations can improve retrieval of tactical situations
that ordinary semantic or metadata retrieval handles poorly.
