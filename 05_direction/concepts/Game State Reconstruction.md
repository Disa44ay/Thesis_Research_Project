# Concept: Game State Reconstruction

Game State Reconstruction maps football participants from video into a structured pitch representation with locations and identities or roles.

For this thesis search, GSR is important primarily as an **enabling representation**. It can support tactical reasoning, retrieval, anticipation, and visualization without requiring the thesis contribution to solve every low-level vision subproblem from scratch.
