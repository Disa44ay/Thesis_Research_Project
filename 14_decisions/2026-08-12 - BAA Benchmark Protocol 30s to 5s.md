# 2026-08-12: BAA Benchmark Protocol 30s to 5s

Current benchmark direction:

Observe 30 seconds → predict all ball actions in the following 5 seconds.

This preserves the multi-action anticipation character of established BAA research and avoids incorrectly reducing the problem to single-label next-action prediction.
