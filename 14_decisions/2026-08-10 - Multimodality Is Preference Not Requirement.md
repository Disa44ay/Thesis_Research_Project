# Decision: Multimodality Is a Preference, Not a Hard Requirement

## Decision
Prefer meaningful multimodal football work, but do not reject a stronger unimodal topic solely because it is unimodal.

## Reason
The project wants contemporary publication-relevant work, while the instructor's stronger disciplinary preference is Computer Vision rather than multimodality itself.

## Guardrail
A second modality must solve a real limitation or contribute measurable information. Decorative multimodality is not enough.
