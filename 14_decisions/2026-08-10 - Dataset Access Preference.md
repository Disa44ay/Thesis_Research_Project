# Decision: Prefer Immediate Public Dataset Access

## Decision
Directly downloadable public datasets are preferred. Free academic datasets requiring registration or requests remain acceptable when access delay is low risk and a fallback exists.

## Reason
The thesis schedule is too short to depend on uncertain approval timing.

## Rejection condition
Reject data sources that require payment, private partnerships, uncertain licenses, or approval delays that can block execution.
