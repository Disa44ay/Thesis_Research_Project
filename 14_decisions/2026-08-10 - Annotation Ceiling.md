# Decision: Cap Manual Annotation at 100 to 150 Samples

## Decision
Allow only a small manually annotated layer, with an absolute practical ceiling around 100 to 150 samples.

## Reason
Although the formal team has three members, only two can be assumed to sustain continuous research work.

## Appropriate uses
Targeted evaluation, tactical-query relevance, robustness cases, or a narrow validation set.

## Inappropriate use
Building a new large training dataset.
